# Logo

## Current state (2026-11-23)

`public/logo.png` – blue circle with a white «P» and a long shadow – was downloaded from the internet. We have
**no licence** for it. Typical stock-icon licences (Flaticon/Freepik and similar) allow use with attribution or
with a paid plan, but **forbid using the icon as a logo or trademark**, so even a paid licence does not make
it ours. It can stay on the site for now, but it should be replaced before the brand is used more widely
(print, packaging, trademark).

## Options

1. **Original mark in the same spirit** – `public/logo-alt.svg`: a white «P» on a blue rounded square, drawn
   from scratch for POSMTRADE as a piece of folded cardboard (die-cut counter, folded corner at the foot). It
   keeps the colours and the feeling people know. Switch = change `logo` in `src/content/site.json`
   to `/logo-alt.svg`.
2. **Designer-made logo** – brief a designer (keep blue + «P»), get the source files and a written transfer
   of rights.
3. Keep the downloaded icon – only if its original author and licence are found and the licence allows logo use.

Whatever is chosen, the same file is used in the site, the agents' e-mail avatars stay unchanged.
